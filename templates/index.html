06 ACCELERATORS

Spend time, gems
and money once.

The fastest route is an allocation problem. Every resource has one early job.

GEMS

VIP 6 → 10 → 12

Reach permanent second builder first. Then time large VIP-point purchases with More Than Gems. VIP 10/12 starts the daily gold-head engine; Books of Covenant become the next hard gate.

Wheel rule: unlock only, unless the commander is your fully funded project.
GOLD HEADS

Save—or fund one planned donor

Either bank universals, or deliberately invest in one high-value early legend with a commander-swap exit strategy. YSG can deliver KvK1/KvK2 AoE, chaining, long-term utility, and possible donor value.

Never scatter heads across mediocre 5511 projects. VERIFY CURRENT IN-GAME SWAP RULES before committing hundreds of sculptures.
UNIVERSAL SPEEDUPS

Critical path only

Use on City Hall/Academy gates and long military research after alliance helps, rune, title, and relevant event overlap. Specialized speedups go first.

Never speed what the alliance can help finish for free.
ACTION POINTS

Natural now; bottles on value

Keep natural AP below cap, use Peacekeeping talents and consecutive attacks, and save bottles for valuable event overlaps unless immediate progression value is greater.

For active min-maxers, AoE chaining turns one initiated attack into several barbarian kills.

AP / COMMANDER XP PLAYBOOK

Make every initiated attack do more.

AoE barbarian chaining means paying AP to attack one barbarian, positioning an AoE commander so the skill hits nearby barbarians, then letting those extra targets aggro without initiating separate AP-costing attacks. Kill the group for the original engagement's AP cost. This is a major reason YSG can create account-development value beyond PvP.

MIN–MAX CALLOUTChaining is manual and positioning-sensitive. Emphasize it when actively optimizing; casual players can prioritize reliable Peacekeeping runs.

01 / EQUIPMENT

Finish sets. Then expand.

Prioritize health and defense efficiency, then the accessories that make the march's engine work. Random legendary pieces are not a build.

  • First complete setOne field march that can stay out and trade.
  • AccessoriesRing/Horn or pairing-specific options before a weak new set.
  • Iconic & critsSteady awakening upgrades; don't chase special talent before roster depth.

02 / FORMATIONS

Match the damage engine.

The modern march is commander + formation + inscriptions. The wrong formation can erase the reason you bought the commander.

  • PincerDefault path for Smite-heavy infantry such as Sun Prime/Bai Qi.
  • WedgeDefault skill-damage home for QSH and debuff archers; also useful for combo cavalry.
  • Arch / alternativesRoster-specific. Use only when the kit and inscriptions beat your Wedge/Pincer set.

03 / CRYSTAL TECH

Economy first, fighting second.

Crystal Tech is temporary and can outweigh commander gaps. Unlock the crystal-income engine early, then fund only the troop branches you field.

  • OpeningCrystal mine, research center, economy and acquisition nodes.
  • CombatUniversal combat value, march cap, then chosen troop branches.
  • Low spenderTwo or three well-teched marches beat five under-teched marches.

10 BATTLEFIELD CONTROL

Good reports are
piloted.

01

Enter together

Let tanks make contact, then layer AoE. A staggered murderball feeds isolated marches one by one.

02

Focus value

Hit exposed skill-damage marches, red-health targets, and overextended cavalry—not the nearest tank by default.

03

Refresh early

Leave before your march becomes slow, focused, and expensive. A clean refresh protects troops and returns pressure faster.

04

Read the report

Separate severely wounded, damage dealt, damage taken, buffs, tech, and target count. Kill points alone conceal bad trades.

11 METHODOLOGY

A snapshot,
not scripture.

The guide prioritizes sources updated in 2026, cross-checks published tier lists against recent player discussions, and distinguishes open-field value from rally, garrison, Canyon, and PvE value.

Pairing ranks are editorial synthesis. They assume comparable expertise, correct troop composition, serviceable legendary gear, relevant armaments, and similar tech. Investment advice additionally depends on sculptures, existing progress, available deputies, account age, spend level, swap expectations, and whether you can actually finish the project. Your account—not the tier list—is the constraint.
